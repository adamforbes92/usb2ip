Dashboard layout

Arrange as Type on: the dashboard splits into Basic, Ignition, Knock, Injection and Advanced sections. Off: one flat grid, and you can drag a dial to reorder it (press and hold first on a phone). Auto-connect presses Launch Gauges for you whenever the page opens.

Channels

Tick a channel to put it on the dashboard. Only ticked channels are decoded and sent, so leaving the list mostly off keeps things quick. The ECU streams 384 channels, named and scaled exactly as Ignitron's own log viewer shows them. Four (AFR, USB timing, log timestamp) are worked out by the PC software rather than sent by the ECU, so they're listed as not sent by the ECU.

Fault codes & limp mode

The chips come from the live stream while gauges are launched. Read fetches the ECU's stored fault memory the way Ignitron's Fault codes window does (code, RPM and load when it was set, the fault's own value, how often and for how long); Clear wipes it and commits, again exactly as Ignitron does. Both need the gauges launched and no PC attached over USB/IP.

Data logger

Record any of the ECU's channels — this list is independent of the dashboard, so you can log every channel while showing 16 dials. On the board writes a compact log to the board's own flash (saved as it goes; survives closing this page) and you download it as CSV afterwards. In this browser keeps the rows here and saves a CSV when you stop (or streams straight to a file where the browser allows it). Gauges must be launched for data to flow.

flash --

Tip: for lots of channels use a low rate. Engine temperatures and pressures barely move between samples, so 1–2 Hz across the whole catalogue costs a few KB a minute, while 20 Hz is for a handful of fast channels (RPM, MAP, knock, lambda). The estimate above updates as you change the selection and rate.

Channels to log

Logs on the board

CSV is built from the stored rows when you download it, using the same names, units and scaling as the dashboard. Raw is the board's binary format. Logs stay on the board until deleted — export what you want to keep, then clear.

Parameters

Export saves your choices — dashboard gauges, layout, hero dial, and the logging channel set and rate — to a small JSON file. Import applies one, so a setup can be moved between boards or kept as a backup.

WiFi access point

The network this device broadcasts. Leave the password blank for an open network; a password, if set, must be at least 8 characters. Saving reboots the device.

WiFi channel

The bridge needs only a few Mbit/s, but every lost WiFi frame stalls the USB stream, so a quiet channel beats a fast one. Scan the band, pick the emptiest of 1 / 6 / 11 (the non-overlapping ones), then apply. Scanning pauses the link for ~2 s — don't do it mid-transfer. Applying reboots.

Press Scan to see what's on the air.

USB routing

VBUS auto-detect is unreliable on this board, so pick where the ECU (USB-A) is connected. Auto follows USB-C detection; USB-C forces the laptop route; WiFi forces the on-board bridge. Forced routes stay powered (no idle shutdown).

Bridge behaviour

With auto power-off on, the ECU 5 V rail cuts after 5 minutes with no activity (the timer shows in Diagnostics). Turn it off to keep the ECU powered permanently.

Factory reset

Restore defaults: open AP IgnitronUSB with no password, at 192.168.1.1. From the board: hold BOOT for 3 s until the LED strobes, release, then press it again.

System

ECU firmware

Bootloader and firmware versions of the ECU's three processors, as Ignitron's firmware manager lists them. Read at session start, whether the PC or this board opened the session; -- until an ECU session has started.

Diagnostics

USB connectivity

USB device is whatever is plugged into the USB-A port (the ECU or, here, a USB disk). USB/IP client is a computer that has attached that device over WiFi so it appears as a local USB port. Capture client is a tool connected to the raw-frame tap port, recording USB traffic while the gauges keep running.

USB capture

Records the raw USB traffic between this board and the ECU, in the format the tools/ scripts read (e.g. to re-verify the channel map against an Ignitron log, or to look at a session handshake). Start, drive (or work through the Ignitron screens), add a note whenever something changes ("idle", "WOT 3rd", "AC on"), then stop and Download the .bin. Nothing here touches the gauges or the bridge; it just copies what already flows. Your phone or laptop keeps the recording, so stay on this page while it runs.
